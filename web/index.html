        What Errand does

        1. It finds the buttons and images your screen reader can't name and names them from what's on screen.
        2. Say "pay my bill" and it works through the site, telling you what it sees.
        3. It stops before paying, sending or deleting, and it never types passwords or card numbers.

        This demo runs on four made-up websites, so nothing real is paid or booked.
